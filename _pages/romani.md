Romani has been at the centre of my research for many years. My work combines fieldwork on present-day dialects, the study of historical sources, and the creation of corpora, dictionaries and other resources that make Romani data accessible to other researchers.

### Dialect documentation and description

I work primarily on Romani varieties of Eastern Europe: in Russia, Lithuania, Ukraine and Moldova, and more recently in North Macedonia.

- ***A grammar, dictionary and texts of Lithuanian Romani*** (in preparation)
- ***A grammar of Russian Romani*** (in preparation)
- **Dictionary of Kišenevare Romani** (*Словарь языка цыган-кишинёвцев*) (in preparation)
- **Servicko Romani:** an edition of a manuscript with vocabulary (in preparation)

### Soviet Romani of the 1920s–1930s

One of my first Romani projects, which I began as a PhD student in Moscow, was a corpus of texts published in Romani in the Soviet Union in the 1920s and 1930s. The original version of the corpus is still available on [web-corpora.net](http://web-corpora.net/RomaniCorpus). Its morphological annotation was done automatically with [uniparser-soviet-romani](https://github.com/burushona/uniparser-soviet-romani), a parser available on my GitHub.

Data from this corpus have been used, for example, in [my paper on the development of the infinitive in Russian Romani](https://brill.com/view/journals/jlc/17/4/article-p703_4.xml).

### Historical sources

Since the early 2010s, I have been collecting unpublished sources on Romani in Russian and Lithuanian archives. This work resulted in the monograph *Romani in sources of the 18th–19th centuries (from the archives of Russia and Lithuania)* (*Цыганский язык в источниках XVIII–XIX вв. (из архивов России и Литвы)*), published in Russian in 2025. The list of Northeastern Romani word forms attested in archival sources up to the early 20th century, which is published in the book, is also available as a [CLDF dataset](https://github.com/burushona/Northeastern-Romani-in-archival-sources) on my GitHub and as a [searchable website](https://burushona.github.io/Northeastern-Romani-in-archival-sources/).

### Etymological dictionary

Together with Mikhail Oslon, I am working on an etymological dictionary of Romani. A [draft](https://rromanes.org/pub/%D0%AD%D0%A1%D0%A6%D0%AF.pdf) is regularly updated online.
