This project collects and analyses early written sources on Romani preserved in archives in Moscow and Saint Petersburg: word lists, short texts and other records from the 18th and 19th centuries. These materials document Romani varieties of the Russian Empire long before systematic dialectological research and are an important source for the history of Romani dialects in Eastern Europe.

The results have been published in a book on Romani sources from 18th- and 19th-century Russian and Lithuanian archives (2025).
