In the 1920s and 1930s, a large body of literature was published in Romani in the Soviet Union: textbooks, fiction, translations and periodicals in a standardised language based mainly on the North Russian Romani dialect. This project builds an annotated corpus of these texts, making them available for research on the grammar, vocabulary and standardisation of Romani as well as on Soviet Romani literature and language policy.
